This map is intended for new and returning Maine Maritime Academy students who are curious about where they can and cannot park in town, although everyone in Castine can use it. This is an interactive visualization of the Traffic Ordinance of the Town of Castine, Maine (2026). The full document can be found here. This is a tool and is not meant to be comprehensive. It will be updated for winter parking.

Key:

  • Red: No Parking Anytime
  • Green: 24-hour Parking
  • Yellow: No Parking 8 am - 4 pm Weekdays
  • Blue: 4-hour Parking Weekdays
  • Purple: No Overnight Parking
  • Pink: 2-hour Parking Weekdays
  • Orange: 1-hour Parking
  • Brown: 15-minute Parking

Some town-wide provisions:

  • Corner parking is prohibited within 10 feet, or 25 feet where posted (Article 2.22)
  • Parking to obstruct fire hydrants, public sidewalks, crosswalks, driveways or roads is prohibited (Article 2.22)
  • Parking at a distance greater than 12 inches from the curb is prohibited (Article 2.22)
  • Double parking is prohibited (Article 2.22)
  • The speed limit on all town streets unless otherwise posted is 25 miles/hour (Article 3.1)
  • Obey stop signs (Article 4.1)

Provisions regarding long-term parking:

  • 24-hour parking is not the same as long-term parking. You must move your car after 24 hours.
  • Parking in an otherwise unrestricted spot for more than 24 hours on Main Street from Court to Water or on Water Street from Pleasant to Green is prohibited (Article 2.25)
  • Parking in an otherwise unrestricted spot for more than 96 hours on any other town street or property is prohibited (Article 2.25)

Notice regarding parking enforcement:

  • The town will be stricter when enforcing the parking ordinance during the 2026-27 school year, which is well within their authority (Article 5.3). This is especially true for illegal parking at the town dock and on Pleasant Street
  • Violation of the ordinance is punishable by ticket or impoundment (Articles 5.1 & 5.2)
  • Regular tickets are $50, snow removal tickets are $100, and handicap tickets are $200. These tickets are stackable, and an additional $25 is levied if you don’t pay them within 30 days (Article 5.1)
  • Your car will be towed (Article 5.2)

Enjoy your time in Castine!
